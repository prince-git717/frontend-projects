About me!

Obito Uchiha was one of the most tragic and complex characters in the Naruto series. He was originally a cheerful and kind-hearted ninja of the Uchiha clan who dreamed of becoming Hokage and protecting his friends. As a child, Obito deeply cared about teamwork and believed that friendship was more important than rules. However, his life changed after a deadly mission where he was believed to have died while saving Kakashi Hatake.
